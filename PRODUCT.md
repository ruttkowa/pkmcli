# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Keyboard-first knowledge workers who keep durable Markdown notes locally and
often work inside terminal-centric environments such as Neovim, tmux, or
Zellij. They need to capture, organize, retrieve, and review knowledge without
leaving a fast text workflow or surrendering ownership of their files.

## Product Purpose

pkm is a navigation and knowledge-management layer over a local Markdown
vault. It makes Inbox-to-PARA organization, projects, metadata folders,
templates, tasks, journal notes, search, and recovery coherent while leaving
editing to the user's chosen external editor. Success means the interface
disappears into the workflow and the vault remains portable, inspectable, and
Git-friendly.

## Brand Personality

Focused, dependable, and quietly expressive. It should feel like a mature
terminal tool: dense enough for experts, legible enough to orient immediately,
and restrained enough that note content remains primary.

## Anti-references

- Multi-hue decoration where colors have no stable meaning.
- Loud dashboards, ornamental gradients, glass effects, or novelty terminal
  aesthetics.
- Mouse-first interfaces, nested modal workflows, and unfamiliar controls that
  interrupt keyboard flow.
- Visual hierarchy that depends on color alone or makes muted text unreadable.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve flow: common actions stay keyboard-direct and state changes are
   visible without ceremony.
2. Make orientation obvious: one accent marks the active location while
   breadcrumbs and neutral hierarchy explain context.
3. Keep semantics honest: reserve additional hues for real status meaning, not
   decoration.
4. Respect the artifact: Markdown files, metadata, and Git remain transparent
   and portable.
5. Prefer earned familiarity: established terminal interaction patterns beat
   invented affordances.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain at least WCAG AA contrast for normal text and clear focus in every
theme. Never rely on hue alone for hierarchy or status; pair color with text,
position, or glyphs. Support both dark and light terminal environments, narrow
layouts, keyboard-only use, and reduced visual noise.
